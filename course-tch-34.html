About this module

This becomes real the moment it has an actual pilot and an actual roadmap.

Everything built across this entire pathway now converges into the practical work of implementation, designing an actual pilot program and a realistic transformation roadmap for a real school, not a hypothetical one. This module walks a teacher through that design process directly, from choosing a scoped starting point to sequencing the stages of change in a way an actual school community can genuinely sustain.

What you'll explore

Five shifts this module is building toward.

  • How to scope a pilot program small enough to be sustainable and real enough to matter.
  • A realistic sequence of stages for a multi-year transformation roadmap, rather than an all-at-once overhaul.
  • What buy-in a pilot actually needs from staff, families, and leadership to survive its first year.
  • A first, concrete pilot design for this teacher's own school.
  • What this teacher has learned across this entire pathway that now shapes how they'll lead implementation.

Three lanes of knowing

Understood, practiced, and explored.

Lane 1 · Science

Why pilots succeed or fail

What's understood about why scoped, well-sequenced pilots sustain real institutional change better than all-at-once overhauls.

Lane 2 · Practice

A pilot-design practice

A short, concrete practice sketching one real, scoped pilot program this teacher could realistically launch at their own school.

Lane 3 · Meaning-making

What have I actually learned across this whole pathway?

Reflection consolidating this teacher's full journey across all four levels into what now shapes their implementation approach.

Outcome

"I have a real, scoped pilot design ready to bring to my own school."

About this module

A school doesn't end at its own front door.

This closing module of Level 3 widens the frame one final time, consolidating the systems thinking, culture design, and partnership work of this entire level into a single view of the school as an ecosystem, interdependent with the families, policies, community institutions, and technologies surrounding it. A teacher completing this level leaves not with a checklist of separate initiatives but with one coherent picture of the school as a living, interconnected whole they are now equipped to help design.

What you'll explore

What this module consolidates.

  • How family, policy, community, and technology function as one interdependent ecosystem around a school.
  • How this level's full arc, adult dynamics, adaptive capacity, creative culture, systems and partnership, now consolidates into one view.
  • What becomes possible once a school is designed as an ecosystem rather than managed as a set of separate initiatives.
  • Where this teacher's own school currently treats its ecosystem as fragmented, and what unifying it could look like.
  • A first, grounded articulation of what kind of school designer this teacher is becoming.

What kind of school designer am I becoming?

Outcome

"I can see and design for my school as one living ecosystem, not a set of separate parts."

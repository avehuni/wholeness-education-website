About this module

A depleted teacher cannot keep showing up regulated.

Teaching asks for a volume of emotional labor that few other professions demand, absorbing a full room's worth of needs every single day, and without real boundaries that labor quietly converts into the kind of depletion that makes everything taught in this pathway harder to access. This module treats boundary-setting not as a sign of lesser commitment but as the specific practice that keeps a teacher able to offer presence over an entire career rather than just a single good year.

What you'll explore

Three shifts this module is building toward.

  • Why unmanaged emotional labor leads predictably to burnout, regardless of how committed a teacher is.
  • A few concrete boundaries that protect a teacher's capacity without reducing their care.
  • One boundary this teacher is ready to set this term.

Three lanes of knowing

Understood, practiced, and explored.

Lane 1 · Science

The cost of unmanaged emotional labor

What's understood about how sustained emotional labor without boundaries predicts burnout, even in highly committed teachers.

Lane 2 · Practice

A boundary-setting practice

A short practice for identifying and stating one concrete boundary clearly, without over-explaining or apologizing for it.

Lane 3 · Meaning-making

Where am I currently giving past my own capacity?

Reflection on where this teacher's current boundaries are thinnest, and what that's costing them.

Outcome

"I have one real boundary in place that protects my capacity to keep showing up."

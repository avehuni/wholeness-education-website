About this module

Gathering four years into one integrated whole.

This module steps back from any single skill or theme to look at the entire four-year arc at once: the self-awareness built in Year 1, the agency practiced in Year 2, the contribution offered in Year 3, and the identity work of Year 4, all gathered into a single, integrated picture of a whole person. Each year's work depends on and strengthens the others, which is the actual shape of the wholeness this entire program has been building toward.

What they'll explore

Five shifts this module is building toward.

  • How the self-awareness, agency, contribution, and identity work of all four years connect to and depend on each other.
  • A way of seeing their own development as one integrated whole, rather than four separate phases.
  • Which of the four years' work still feels most available to them, and which could use continued attention.
  • A personal map of the whole framework as they've actually lived it.
  • A simple way of returning to this map later, as life keeps adding new material to it.

Three lanes of knowing

Understood, practiced, and explored.

Lane 1 · Science

Why integration is the actual goal

What's understood about how separate capacities, once integrated, function more powerfully together than any one does alone.

Lane 2 · Practice

Mapping the whole framework

A guided exercise connecting the major work of all four years into one personal, integrated map.

Lane 3 · Meaning-making

What does my own wholeness actually look like?

A reflective exercise picturing the four years' work as one connected whole, rather than four separate chapters.

Outcome

"I can see four years of work as one integrated whole, and I know how each part supports the others."
